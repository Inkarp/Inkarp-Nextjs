Inkarp Instruments is the authorised distributor and service partner for Hitachi High-Tech thermal analysis systems in India. The NEXTA DMA200 Dynamic Mechanical Analyzer measures how a material's stiffness and damping change with temperature, time and frequency — revealing the glass transition, modulus, viscoelastic behaviour and mechanical performance that a single thermal curve cannot show. From carbon-fibre composites to rubbers, films and adhesives, the DMA200 characterises both very stiff and very soft materials on one platform.

Guidance Mode walks non-experts through method setup and analysis, while master-curve (time–temperature superposition) and activation-energy calculations come as standard. Overview

Dynamic Mechanical Analysis (DMA) applies a small oscillating force to a sample and measures the resulting deformation. From the amplitude and phase of the response the instrument derives the storage modulus (E′ — elastic stiffness), loss modulus (E″ — viscous dissipation) and loss tangent (tan δ — damping). Running these while scanning temperature or frequency exposes the glass transition, secondary relaxations, cure, softening and the frequency dependence of mechanical properties.

The NEXTA DMA200 uses an LVDT displacement sensor and a spring-mounted drive shaft, so no compressed-air supply is needed. A 20 N maximum force combined with 5×10⁻⁶ N force resolution lets one instrument span from rigid carbon-fibre laminates to soft gels. The Real View camera records the sample throughout the run, linking every image to the curve by timestamp.

Value in one line: one analyzer for stiff and soft materials, with guided method setup, standard TTS/master-curve analysis and Inkarp service across India.
